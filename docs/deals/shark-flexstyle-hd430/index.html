Shark FlexStyle Air Styling and Drying System deal

A hair dryer that twists into a styling wand, with auto-wrap curlers that spin both ways. The Airwrap idea at a much friendlier cost. Several bundles exist, so check which one the deal covers.

Published Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

By ViewDeals Desk

See deal at Amazon →

The Shark FlexStyle is the hair tool to look at if you like the idea of a Dyson Airwrap but not the bill. A dryer head twists into a styling wand, and the two auto-wrap curling barrels spin in opposite directions, so you can curl both sides of your head toward or away from your face without switching hands.

Shark says it checks the air temperature a thousand times a second to limit heat damage, and at just over a pound and a half it’s light enough to hold up through a full blowout. It’s marketed for straight, wavy, curly and coily hair, and it comes in several different bundles.

That last part is the catch. Amazon lists versions with anywhere from four to seven attachments, so check exactly which bundle the deal applies to before you buy, and skip any listing that isn’t sold by Amazon or Shark. It is corded, which is normal for a dryer but worth knowing if you hoped to style away from an outlet. If you only need fast drying and straightening, Walmart’s simpler Shark Glossi costs less and does less.

Who it’s for
Anyone who blow-dries and styles most days and wants one tool instead of three.
Skip it if
You air-dry and only style for special occasions.
