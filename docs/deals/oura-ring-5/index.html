Oura Ring 5 deal

The smallest Oura yet: all titanium, with sleep, heart-rate and temperature tracking and close to a week per charge. It needs a paid membership, and you should use the sizing kit first.

Published Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

By ViewDeals Desk

See deal at Best Buy →

The Oura Ring 5 is the smallest Oura yet, and it is on sale at Best Buy through Saturday, November 28, with the same deal at Target and Amazon. Oura’s own site is running its discount through November 30 if you miss the Best Buy window.

It’s an all-titanium ring that tracks sleep, heart rate, temperature trends and activity, with Best Buy listing more than 50 health measurements in all. Battery life is rated at six to nine days per charge, far longer than most smartwatches, and the ring is designed to be worn around the clock, including in bed, which is where most of its useful data comes from.

Two things to know before you buy. First, most features need a paid Oura membership, so budget for that on top of the ring. Second, use the included sizing kit before you choose a size. Oura sizes don’t match standard US ring sizes and they differ from older Oura generations, so even your old Oura size may be wrong. Pick the size and finish on the product page.

Who it’s for
People who care about sleep and recovery but don’t want a watch on their wrist.
Skip it if
You don’t want another subscription, or you need on-screen notifications.
