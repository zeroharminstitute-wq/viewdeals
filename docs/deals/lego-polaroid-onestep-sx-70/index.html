LEGO Ideas Polaroid OneStep SX-70 Camera (21345) deal

A life-size LEGO model of the classic instant camera. Press the red shutter and a little illustrated photo slides out. TODAY cites price tracking calling this its lowest price. A gift for grown-ups, not kids.

By ViewDeals Desk

See deal at Amazon →

This is the gift for the adult who already owns enough candles. The LEGO Ideas Polaroid OneStep SX-70 is a 516-piece, life-size model of the classic instant camera, and TODAY cites price-tracking data calling this its lowest price yet on Amazon.

The clever part is that it works like the real thing, sort of. Press the red shutter button and one of three illustrated LEGO photos slides out of the front. The set also comes with a booklet of interviews with the fan designer who pitched the idea and the LEGO designers who finished it, which makes it a good pick for photography nerds and design fans as much as for LEGO collectors.

At 516 pieces it is a manageable build, so it suits someone new to adult LEGO sets as well as a regular builder. It’s rated 18+ and made for a shelf, not a playroom, and to be clear, it doesn’t take pictures. Check the set number, 21345, so you get the right model and not a lookalike.

Who it’s for
Photography fans, design lovers and adults who like a calm evening build.
Skip it if
You’re shopping for a child, or for someone who wants a camera that actually shoots.
