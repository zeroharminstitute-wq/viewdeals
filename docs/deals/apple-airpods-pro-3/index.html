Apple AirPods Pro 3 deal

Amazon · Tech

By ViewDeals Desk

Published Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

Apple’s current flagship earbuds, with stronger noise cancellation, heart-rate sensing for workouts and Live Translation. NBC Select’s tracking has Amazon’s price at its lowest yet.

AirPods Pro 3 are Apple’s current flagship earbuds, and this is the deepest cut on them we’ve seen during Black Friday week. NBC Select’s price tracking puts Amazon’s price at the lowest it has recorded, and both NBC Select and TODAY found Amazon cheaper than Target and Best Buy on the same model this week.

The upgrades over the AirPods Pro 2 are real rather than cosmetic. Apple says the noise cancellation removes up to twice as much noise, the buds now read your heart rate during workouts, and Live Translation works in conversation when paired with a recent iPhone. Battery life is rated at up to eight hours with noise cancellation on, and the box includes five ear-tip sizes, which matters more for comfort than any spec sheet.

Before you buy, check that the listing says AirPods Pro 3 and that it is shipped and sold by Amazon rather than a third-party seller. If you don’t care about noise cancellation, the regular AirPods 4 at Walmart cost much less and are fine for calls and podcasts.
