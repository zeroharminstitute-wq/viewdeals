TCL 65” S4CF Series 4K UHD HDR Smart TV with Fire TV (2026) deal

A 2026 TCL 65-inch 4K TV with Fire TV built in, a Best Buy Doorbuster at $229.99. It’s a budget set, but it’s hard to get this much screen for less.

By ViewDeals Desk

Best Buy has TCL’s 65-inch S4CF, the 2026 model, as a Doorbuster at $229.99, down from a comparable value of $469.99. That is a very low price for a screen this size from a brand with a long record in budget TVs.

This TV suits a guest room, a basement, a dorm or a first apartment, anywhere the goal is a big picture without a big bill. Fire TV is built in, so you get the main streaming apps without a separate stick, and voice control works through the remote.

Be realistic about what this money buys. The S4 is TCL’s entry series. It is not a QLED or mini-LED set, so it will not get as bright or show as much contrast as TCL’s higher series, and it is not aimed at fast competitive gaming. If you watch a lot of movies in a dark room, you will notice the difference. Before you check out, confirm the model says S4CF and 2026, check that the listing is sold by Best Buy, and measure your wall or stand: a 65-inch TV is about 57 inches wide.

If you want better picture quality, step up to a QLED or mini-LED model, which costs more. For a gaming setup in the same room, the Nintendo Switch 2 bundle is also a Best Buy Doorbuster this week.

Who it’s for
Anyone who wants a big 4K screen for a spare room, dorm or first apartment on a tight budget.
Skip it if
You watch movies in a dark room and care most about contrast and brightness.
