Fine, it’s another robot vacuum. It’s also one of the better-value self-emptying models this season, and NBC Select’s price tracking has it at the lowest price it has seen on Amazon. The dock empties the robot into a sealed 2.7-liter bag, which Amazon says lasts seven to nine weeks between changes.

It maps your home with LiDAR, so it cleans in tidy rows instead of bouncing off furniture, and you can mark rooms and no-go zones in the app. Roborock lists suction at 10,000Pa, and the main brush is designed to resist tangling, which is the difference between a robot you use every day and one you rescue from pet hair every other day. It also mops, with three water-flow settings.

Treat the mopping as a bonus for light upkeep on hard floors rather than a replacement for a real mop on sticky spills. Check the model name on the listing says Q7 M5+, because Roborock sells several similar-looking Q-series robots and the cheaper ones skip the self-emptying dock.
