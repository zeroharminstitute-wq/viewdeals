Six Black Friday Gifts We’d Actually Give

Six gifts on sale this week that we’d be happy to wrap, with a straight answer on who each one suits and what to check before you buy.

Published Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

By ViewDeals Desk

Save this guide to Pinterest

How we chose these

Every gift here is on sale this Black Friday week, and each has its own full write-up on ViewDeals, so you can read the details before you commit. We left out anything that only makes sense as a gift if you already know the person’s exact model, size or setup.

We grouped them by the kind of person you’re shopping for, because “best gift” means very little without that.

For the commuter or frequent flyer

If they use an iPhone, AirPods Pro 3 are the safe bet. The noise cancellation is a real step up from the previous generation, the buds track heart rate during workouts, and both NBC Select and TODAY found Amazon’s price on them the best of the big retailers this week.

If they’d rather have over-ear headphones, or they use Android, the Sony WH-1000XM5 is the better gift. It has been replaced by the XM6, which is exactly why Best Buy has it as a Doorbuster through November 28, and the noise cancelling is still among the best you can buy.

For the person who styles their hair every day

The Shark FlexStyle dries and then curls or smooths with the same tool, and its two curling barrels spin in opposite directions so both sides of the head are easy. It comes in several bundles, so check which attachments the deal includes before you buy.

For the adult who has everything

The LEGO Ideas Polaroid OneStep SX-70 is a life-size model of the classic instant camera. Press the shutter and a little illustrated photo slides out. It’s 516 pieces, rated 18+, and makes a calm evening’s build for anyone who likes photography or design. It doesn’t take real pictures, which is worth saying on the gift tag.

For a busy household

The Roborock Q7 M5+ is a self-emptying robot vacuum with LiDAR mapping and a brush designed to resist tangling on pet hair. It’s a gift for the household rather than one person, so it suits a family or a couple who already argue about whose turn it is to vacuum.

For the sleep tracker

The Oura Ring 5 is the smallest Oura yet and lasts about a week per charge. Two cautions make it a gift to give carefully: most features need a paid membership, and you need the right size. Give the sizing kit first, or give it to someone who has already said they want one.

Before you wrap anything

Buy from the retailer itself rather than a third-party seller on its marketplace, so returns are simple. Check the return window, since gifts often get opened weeks after purchase. And move quickly on the Best Buy deals: its Black Friday event ends November 28, while the Amazon deals here run through November 30.
