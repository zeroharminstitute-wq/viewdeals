How we pick and check every deal

Updated Nov 25, 2026

Every morning we read the retailers’ own deal pages and a short list of reputable roundups, and we only consider offers reported in the last 48 hours.

A deal has to pass four checks: it’s from an established retailer, a recent source confirms the sale, the product is well known or well reviewed, and it isn’t something we already list.

Our write-ups say who a product suits, who should skip it, and what to check before you buy, such as the model year, the size or the seller. We write every word ourselves and never copy a retailer’s listing.

Deals come down when they end. A page for an ended deal stays up with a clear notice and links to live deals, so you never land on a dead end.
